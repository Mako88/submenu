Sweep 034 -- is tau_branch a real improvement, or compensation for a tail?

Sweep 030's control condition produced the largest single-parameter effect
in this project: tau_branch 60 worth +0.218 and 120 worth +0.298, with
binding off entirely. Larger than homeostatic settling's +0.197 and than
every learning rule combined.

But every condition in 030 ran at drain_steps 200, where the baseline is
0.620 against the tail-free 0.802. A 15-step filter forgets everything
across 200 silent steps and a 120-step one does not, so the effect may be
compensation for a handicap the standard setup does not impose. Same shape
as the baseline-shift error sweep 026 already paid for.

These conditions remove the tail and look again, and carry their own link
back to 030 so nothing depends on numbers from another run. tb240 is
predicted to fail -- the task's cues are 100 steps apart and a 240-step
memory cannot separate them.

Prediction, recorded before the run: helps at drain 0, but by +0.02 to
+0.10 rather than +0.20 to +0.30, with an interior optimum near 30-60. So
mostly compensatory and partly real.
