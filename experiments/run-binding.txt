Sweep 030 -- can the latency window be bought, and what does it cost?

Two routes, one matrix, so they are comparable rather than measured a week
apart against different baselines.

  tau_branch 60 / 120   widens the window by changing the forward path's own
                        integration filter. Windows measured at 28.7 and ~37
                        steps against 11.9 at the default. Costs whatever
                        changing the column's computation costs, which is
                        unmeasured and is half of what this sweep prices.

  bind_tau_pre 300      gives the binding rule its own presynaptic trace, so
  + tau_act_fast 300    the forward path is provably untouched. Window measured
                        at 154 steps, with 40% of the update surviving lag 150.

The headline is on-tag-lag150. Two conditions have already failed at that
lag -- on-lag150 at +0.001 (p = 0.88) and on-lag150-slow at +0.000
(p = 1.0000) -- so a win there is a real result and a loss costs a config
flag that ships off by default.
