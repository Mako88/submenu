Sweep 003 -- plastic vs frozen at 24 neurons with a pre-trained readout.
20 seeds, 400 readout-only episodes, then 400 episodes with column plasticity.

Sweeps 001 and 002 both found nothing: -0.003 (p 0.79) with no headroom at 96
neurons, +0.011 (p 0.45) with a +0.193 linear-to-MLP gap at 24. Between them
they rule out the benchmark, since the rule fails equally whether or not there
is room to improve.

That leaves the credit signal. The column is told readout.W^T @ (-err), so it
is only ever as well-directed as the readout is trained, and the readout is a
single-pass delta rule that reaches ~0.72 of the ~0.86 available. A column
faithfully following a half-trained teacher is exactly what both nulls look
like.

This sweep fixes the teacher before blaming the student: the readout trains
alone for 400 episodes with the column frozen, and only then does column
plasticity switch on. Both arms get the same 800 total episodes, so the only
difference is whether the column learns during the second half.

Prediction, recorded before the result: if the teacher was the problem, plastic
should now beat frozen. If this comes back null too, the three-factor rule as
implemented does not work, and the next suspect is the eligibility trace itself
rather than anything feeding it.
