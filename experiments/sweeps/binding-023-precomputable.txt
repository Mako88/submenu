Sweep 023 -- is the settling operating point precomputable?
===========================================================

QUESTION
--------
Sweep 022 established that the +0.197 from homeostatic settling is an operating
point rather than a learned trajectory: a column handed a settled twin's
threshold and knee, with nothing adapting and `lr=0`, reads 0.752 against 0.582.

What it did not establish is that the operating point is *free*. The twin had
fifty episodes of exposure to the task to find it -- unsupervised, no labels,
`lr=0` throughout, but derived from data.

If the values depend only on the input's marginal statistics, they are
precomputable and fifty episodes of every future run are free. If they depend on
the task's temporal structure, they are not, and the more interesting question
becomes what about timing a per-neuron threshold can encode.

CONDITIONS  (20 seeds, 100 episodes, checkpoints every 10, 96 neurons, lr 0)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The column being measured always runs the real task. Only the *twin* that finds
the operating point sees different input.

  all               no preset, everything adapting          (the 022 baseline)
  preset-task       twin on the real task                   (= 022's preset-frozen)
  preset-shuffled   twin on the task's marginals with timing destroyed
  preset-noise      twin on Bernoulli events at the task's overall rate

`shuffled` permutes each channel's time series independently, so per-channel
event count and amplitude distribution are preserved *exactly* while cue timing,
the cue-to-go relationship and cross-channel synchrony are gone. Exactness is
the point: a matched-rate Bernoulli stream alone would confound "homeostasis
does not need timing" with "the rate was not matched closely enough". `noise` is
kept as the weaker version, to show how far down the ladder the answer holds.

A MEASUREMENT BUG THIS SWEEP FOUND, AND WHAT IT COSTS SWEEP 022
----------------------------------------------------------------
The sparsity column reported `column.sparsity`, which returns the firing-rate
EMA. That EMA only advances while `learning` is True, so **at checkpoint 0,
before any training episode has run, it returns `target_rate` unchanged from
initialisation.** Every condition read 0.0300 there. It looked like a
measurement, and it was the initial value of a variable.

Sparsity is now measured during the probe itself, by counting emissions. The
first thing that showed:

  all, episode 0     sparsity 0.0022, not 0.0300

**The untrained column is nearly silent** -- a fifteenth of target rate. So
sweep 022's episode-0 row concealed the largest fact in it: the 0.582 baseline
every mechanism in this project is measured against is a column that is barely
firing.

That does not overturn sweep 022's conclusion, it sharpens it. "The +0.197 is an
operating point" becomes something more specific and more testable: **the
operating point is largely a firing rate, and homeostasis's first job is to
bring a silent column up to target.** Presetting theta and knee supplies that
immediately.

What it does invalidate is any reading of sweep 022's episode-0 sparsity row.
Recorded in AUDIT.md. The rows from episode 10 onward were measured after
training episodes, where the EMA does advance, so they stand.

PREDICTION (recorded before the 20-seed run)
----------------------------------------------
One seed is in hand, as it was for sweeps 020 and 022, and the prediction is
informed by it. Stating that rather than pretending otherwise.

  all               0.582 at episode 0 rising to ~0.76; sparsity 0.002 -> 0.031
  preset-task       0.74 to 0.77, flat            sparsity ~0.020
  preset-shuffled   0.70 to 0.74, flat            sparsity 0.026 to 0.031
  preset-noise      0.62 to 0.68, flat            sparsity 0.010 to 0.018

**The central claim: `preset-shuffled` lands below `preset-task` by 0.02 to
0.06, while running at a firing rate at least as close to target.** That is the
combination that decides it, and the one seed already shows it: shuffled sits at
sparsity 0.0288 against task's 0.0204 -- *better* calibrated -- and decodes
0.722 against 0.833. If it held only at worse sparsity the result would be about
activity level and not about the operating point.

So: **if shuffled is below task at equal-or-better sparsity, the operating point
is not precomputable from marginal input statistics.** It encodes something
about the task's temporal structure, and the "fifty free episodes" idea is dead.

WHAT WOULD REFUTE IT
---------------------
`preset-shuffled` within 0.02 of `preset-task`. Then the operating point depends
only on marginals, the twin can be settled on synthetic input, and every run
starts fifty episodes ahead. That is the outcome I would prefer and the one I
am predicting against.

A CONFOUND NAMED IN ADVANCE
----------------------------
`preset-noise` is expected to run under-active (one seed: 0.0125 against a 0.03
target). If it decodes worst, that is **not** clean evidence about operating
points, because a column at 40% of target rate decodes badly for reasons that
have nothing to do with what its threshold encodes. It is reported as a ladder
rung, not as a third data point, and the argument rests on `shuffled`, where the
sparsity confound runs the *wrong way* for the conclusion.

WHAT THIS SWEEP CANNOT SAY
---------------------------
Nothing here identifies *what* about timing the threshold encodes. A refuted
"precomputable" leaves that wide open, and the honest next step would be to ask
which of theta and knee carries it -- preset one from the task and the other
from shuffled input. Not folded in here.

SETTINGS
--------
20 seeds, 100 episodes, checkpoints every 10, 300 probe episodes, 96 neurons,
column lr 0, binding off, lateral off, `--preset-from 50`.
