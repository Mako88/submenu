Sweep 005 -- sign-only eligibility, 24 neurons, 20 seeds, no pretraining.

This follows directly from the gradient check rather than from guesswork. Over
90 sampled synapses the eligibility trace agrees with the finite-difference
gradient in sign 0.64 of the time (58/90, one-sided p = 0.004) and in magnitude
not at all (correlation -0.045). Since the update is lr * signal * elig, the
magnitude has been contributing noise of the same order as the signal for every
step of every sweep so far.

So: use only the part that carries information. elig_mode="sign" takes the
direction and discards the size, with magnitude retained solely as a
participation gate at one RMS -- a near-zero trace means the synapse was not
involved, and the sign check has nothing to say about those.

Comparable to sweep 002 (24 neurons, no pretraining), which gave +0.011 at
p = 0.45 with magnitude updates.

Prediction, recorded before the result: this is the first change aimed at the
measured defect rather than at its surroundings, so it has a real chance. But
sign-only throws away any notion of *how much* a synapse mattered, which may
simply trade one noise source for another. A null here means the trace needs
rebuilding rather than reinterpreting, and the surrogate h is the suspect.

CONFOUNDED -- this sweep ran before the elig_rms scaling bug was found, so its
  update sizes were mis-scaled like every sweep before it. Re-run after 006.
