Sweep 029 -- can theta be computed instead of found?
====================================================

IN PLAIN TERMS
--------------
Each neuron has to discover its own sensitivity setting, and that discovery is
worth more than every learning rule in this project combined. It takes about
fifty rounds of practice, and the previous sweep showed you cannot shortcut it by
copying settings between neurons -- each one genuinely needs its own.

But a probe just found what the setting depends on, and it is almost embarrassing:
**how fast that neuron forgets.** Neurons in this model are deliberately built
with a wide range of memory spans, and a neuron's sensitivity turns out to be
almost entirely predicted by its own span -- 90% of the variation, from one
number that is fixed when the network is built.

So the setting may not need to be discovered at all. It may just need to be
calculated. This tests whether a network handed calculated settings performs like
one that spent fifty rounds finding them.

QUESTION
--------
Sweep 027 established the operating point is a per-neuron *fit*: permuting theta
between neurons costs 0.130 on 0/20 seeds at p = 0.0000, and the permuted column
fires at double target rate. So sampling from the settled distribution does not
work -- the assignment is what matters.

That leaves one form of "precomputable" standing, and a probe over 6 seeds and
576 neurons found it. Correlating each neuron's settled theta against its own
local properties:

  membrane tau         -0.793 +/- 0.023
  branch gain           0.077 +/- 0.162
  excitatory fraction   0.037 +/- 0.102
  total fan-in weight   0.029 +/- 0.112
  mean conduction delay -0.022 +/- 0.038

**Theta is fitted to the membrane time constant and to essentially nothing
else.** In logs the relationship is tighter still: log theta against log tau
gives r = -0.974, and

    theta = 5.927 * tau^-0.748

explains **90% of the variance** at a median error of 10.6%.

Mechanically that is the right shape. A leaky integrator with unit DC gain
driven by sparse input has membrane fluctuation falling as tau rises, so the
threshold that produces a fixed firing rate must fall with tau. A white-noise
argument predicts an exponent near -0.5; the measured -0.75 is steeper, which
the branch filter and short-term plasticity could account for and which nothing
here has derived. **The exponent is fitted, not explained.**

Crucially this preserves what 027 showed matters: every neuron still gets a
threshold computed from *its own* property, so the assignment is intact. It is
the version of "precomputable" that 027 did not refute.

CONDITIONS  (20 seeds, 100 episodes, 96 neurons, lr 0)
--------------------------------------------------------
  all              settle normally, everything adapting     0.755
  preset-shuffled  settled twin's theta and knee            0.803
  tau-init         theta computed from tau, nothing adapting, knee at init
  tau-init-knee    theta computed from tau, knee preset from a twin
  tau-init-adapt   theta computed from tau, then adaptation left ON

The constants are frozen at the values fitted from the probe above and are not
refitted per run. Refitting would let the initialiser peek at the answer it is
supposed to predict.

PREDICTION (recorded before the run)
--------------------------------------
  tau-init         0.72 to 0.78
  tau-init-knee    0.76 to 0.80
  tau-init-adapt   0.78 to 0.82, and reaching it in far fewer than 50 episodes

**The central claim: `tau-init-knee` lands within 0.04 of `preset-shuffled`'s
0.803, from a formula and a twin's knee rather than from settling theta.**

The reasoning is arithmetic. The fit explains 90% of theta's variance, so a
computed theta should recover most of what a settled one buys. Sweep 027 measured
the knee's separate contribution at +0.041, which is why `tau-init` alone is
predicted lower -- it leaves the knee at its initial value, and 027's
`preset-theta` condition (theta right, knee at init) landed at 0.763.

So `tau-init` should land near 0.763 if the formula is as good as a settled
theta, and materially below it if the missing 10% of variance matters.

WHAT EACH OUTCOME MEANS
-------------------------
  tau-init ~ 0.763    The formula is as good as settling, for theta. Fifty
                      episodes of threshold search become a closed-form
                      expression, at any column size, with no twin and no data.
                      That is the practical result the project has been circling
                      since sweep 022.

  tau-init well below  The missing 10% of variance is load-bearing. Then theta
                      depends on something per-neuron that tau does not capture,
                      and the probe should be re-run against interaction terms
                      rather than the marginals it tested.

  tau-init-adapt fast  Even if the formula is imperfect, starting near the answer
                      should cut the search short. Worth knowing separately,
                      because it is useful even when the formula is not exact.

WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
---------------------
The exponent was fitted at 96 neurons on one task. Whether -0.748 is universal or
a property of this configuration is untested, and the honest version of any
claim here is "a power law in tau, with an exponent that must be refitted per
configuration until something derives it".

SETTINGS
--------
20 seeds, 100 episodes, checkpoints every 10, 300 probe episodes, 96 neurons,
lr 0, binding and lateral off. THETA_A = 5.927, THETA_B = -0.748, frozen in
experiments/trajectory.py.

RESULT -- 20 seeds
------------------
Linear decodability. The `preset-*` and `tau-init*` conditions are frozen in
every adaptation, so they are constant across checkpoints by construction -- what
carries the finding is their *level*, which is a real comparison (the sweep 022
lesson, applied rather than re-learned).

  condition          ep 0    ep 100    sparsity
  all                0.582    0.755     0.030     the settling trajectory
  tau-init           0.757    0.757     0.024     **theta from the formula**
  tau-init-adapt     0.757    0.779     0.030     formula, then allowed to adapt
  tau-init-knee      0.786    0.786     0.023     formula + a twin's knee
  preset-theta       0.763    0.763     0.028     theta from a settled twin
  preset-shuffled    0.803    0.803     0.027     the full settled operating point
  preset-task        0.752    0.752     0.022
  preset-noise       0.691    0.691     0.017
  preset-permuted    0.673    0.673     0.060
  preset-knee        0.566    0.566     0.002

  tau-init       vs preset-theta      -0.006    8/20   p = 0.7672   **null**
  tau-init-knee  vs preset-shuffled   -0.018    6/20   p = 0.1997   null
  tau-init-adapt vs all               +0.024   13/20   p = 0.0678   null

THE FORMULA IS AS GOOD AS SETTLING, FOR THETA
-----------------------------------------------
`tau-init` computes each neuron's threshold as `theta = 5.927 * tau^-0.748` at
construction, from that neuron's own membrane time constant and nothing else --
no data, no twin, no settling. It reaches **0.757**, against **0.763** for
`preset-theta`, which obtains its thresholds by settling an actual twin column
for fifty episodes and copying them over. The paired difference is **-0.006 on
8 of 20 seeds at p = 0.7672.**

**The closed-form expression is indistinguishable from fifty episodes of
homeostatic search.**

Put against the trajectory it replaces, which is the comparison that matters:

  a settling column       0.582 at episode 0  ->  0.755 after 100 episodes
  the formula             0.757 at episode 0, immediately, frozen

The +0.197 that sweep 022 found homeostatic settling to be worth -- the largest
single effect in this project, larger than every learning rule combined -- is
**available at construction time from a one-line function of each neuron's own
membrane constant.**

PREDICTION SCORECARD: THE CENTRAL CLAIM HELD, FIRST TIME
----------------------------------------------------------
  predicted  tau-init        0.72 to 0.78     measured 0.757   in band
  predicted  tau-init-knee   0.76 to 0.80     measured 0.786   in band
  predicted  tau-init-adapt  0.78 to 0.82     measured 0.779   just below

  central claim: "tau-init-knee lands within 0.04 of preset-shuffled's 0.803"
                 measured 0.786, a gap of 0.017                 CONFIRMED

  supporting:    "tau-init should land near 0.763 if the formula is as good as
                 a settled theta"
                 measured 0.757, null against it at p = 0.7672  CONFIRMED

This is the first sweep in the project whose central claim was stated in advance
and came back confirmed. Sweep 027 was the first where a mechanistic prediction
beat the pilot it was checking; this is the first where the prediction was simply
right. Recorded because the previous nineteen sweeps are mostly a record of the
opposite, and the base rate is the point.

WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH, AND THE PART THAT IS STILL A TWIN
---------------------------------------------------------------
**The knee is not computable yet.** `tau-init-knee` reaches 0.786 -- within
0.017 of the full settled operating point, null at p = 0.1997 -- but it gets its
knee by copying a settled twin's. So the honest statement is:

  theta   computable at construction, from tau alone            DONE
  knee    still requires settling or a twin                     OPEN

`preset-knee` at 0.566 with sparsity 0.002 shows why the knee cannot simply be
left alone: a column with the right knee and a default theta is nearly silent and
decodes worse than anything else in the table. The two have to be set together,
and only one of them has a formula.

**Adapting from the formula is not detectably better than adapting from
scratch.** `tau-init-adapt` reaches 0.779 against `all`'s 0.755, +0.024 at
p = 0.0678 -- directionally right, not significant. What it *does* do is start
at 0.757 instead of 0.582, so the hundred episodes buy nothing they were not
already given. The prediction said "reaching it in far fewer than 50 episodes";
the truth is it starts there and then wanders, which is a stronger statement than
the one predicted and worth saying in those terms rather than as a near-miss on
a band.

**And the exponent is still fitted, not derived.** -0.748 came from six seeds at
96 neurons on one task. Whether it is universal or a property of this
configuration is untested, and every claim above should be read as "a power law
in tau whose exponent must be refitted per configuration until something derives
it". That is the first thing to check at another column size, and it is now
cheap: `--theta-from-tau 1` at 48 and 192 neurons against the same references.

WHY THIS MATTERS BEYOND THE NUMBER
------------------------------------
Sweep 023 found the operating point is better obtained from structureless input
than from the task, so it is not acquired. Sweep 025 refuted criticality as the
explanation. Sweep 027 found it is a *per-neuron fit* -- permuting it between
neurons costs 0.130 on 0/20 seeds -- and therefore not a recipe.

Those three together said: per-neuron, not task-specific, not criticality. This
one supplies what was missing -- **the per-neuron quantity it is a function of.**
Each neuron's threshold is a power law in its own membrane time constant, which
is a local, static property fixed at construction. Nothing has to be searched,
nothing has to be shared, and nothing has to run first.

That is also the sharpest answer this project has produced to whether the
homeostatic gain is "learning". It is not. It is a fixed point that a
one-parameter family of neurons has by construction, which the homeostatic loop
finds by search because nobody had told it the answer.
