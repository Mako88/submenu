Sweep 027 -- what do theta and the knee actually encode?
========================================================

IN PLAIN TERMS
--------------
The single biggest effect in this project is not learning. It is each neuron
settling on a sensitivity setting -- two numbers per neuron -- and that turns
out to be worth more than every learning rule combined.

Nobody knows what those numbers are *for*. Two guesses are already dead: it is
not the network poised at a critical point (025), and it is not simply getting
the firing rate right (023).

This asks the sharpest remaining question. Take a settled network and shuffle
the settings between neurons -- same numbers, wrong owners. If it still works,
the settings are just a *recipe* and you could generate them from scratch. If it
falls apart, each neuron genuinely needs its own, matched to the inputs it
happens to have.

The two answers point in completely different directions, which is what makes
this worth running.

QUESTION
--------
Sweep 022 established the +0.197 that homeostatic settling buys is an operating
point rather than a trajectory: a column handed a settled twin's `theta` and
`knee` reads 0.752 against 0.582 with nothing adapting. Sweep 023 established it
is better found on input with the task's timing destroyed (+0.051, p = 0.0032).
Sweep 025 eliminated criticality (m_excess 0.001-0.004, every comparison null,
while decodability moved 0.121).

Two explanations gone, and the object is two vectors of 96 numbers. TODO calls
characterising them the obvious next move, and it is.

CONDITIONS  (20 seeds, 100 episodes, 96 neurons, lr 0, preset from a shuffled-input twin)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  all              no preset, everything adapting          the 022/023 baseline
  preset-both      theta and knee both preset              the 023 result, 0.803
  preset-theta     theta only; knee left at `knee_init`
  preset-knee      knee only; theta left at `threshold_init`
  preset-permuted  both preset, then permuted across neurons

`preset-permuted` is the sweep. One permutation is applied to *both* vectors, so
each neuron receives a matched `(theta, knee)` pair belonging to some other
neuron rather than two unrelated numbers -- mismatching them as well would
confound "wrong owner" with "internally inconsistent", and only the first is the
question. The distribution of both quantities is preserved **exactly**; the only
thing destroyed is which neuron owns which.

PREDICTION (recorded before the run)
--------------------------------------
**One seed says permuting does not hurt -- 0.806 against 0.722 unpermuted -- and
I am predicting against it.**

Saying that plainly because a single seed has now produced three claims that 20
seeds corrected: sweep 020's ordering, sweep 023's sign, and sweep 024's
magnitude, which reached four files as a statement of fact before it was
measured. Running one seed to check the plumbing is fine. Letting it set an
expectation has a 0-for-3 record in this project, so the prediction is made on
mechanism instead.

The mechanism says the assignment should matter. Membrane time constants are
drawn log-uniformly across 12-320ms, fan-in is sampled independently per neuron,
and Dale signs are drawn per source -- so two neurons in the same column see
genuinely different drive distributions. A threshold that is correct for a 300ms
integrator with excitatory-heavy fan-in should be wrong for a 15ms one. If the
settling process is fitting each neuron to its own inputs, permuting must cost
something.

  all              0.755  (established)
  preset-both      0.803  (established, sweep 023)
  preset-permuted  0.70 to 0.76 -- a loss of 0.04 to 0.10 against preset-both
  preset-theta     0.62 to 0.70
  preset-knee      0.62 to 0.70

**The central claim: permuting costs at least 0.04, and neither vector alone
reaches within 0.05 of both together.**

WHAT EACH OUTCOME MEANS
-------------------------
  permuting hurts     The settling is per-neuron fitting. The operating point is
                      not portable and cannot be generated -- it has to be
                      found, per column, against that column's own wiring. The
                      50-episode cost stands and sweep 023's "precomputable" is
                      narrower than it sounded: precomputable from *any input*,
                      but not without the column.

  permuting is free   Then the settling is producing a *distribution*, not an
                      assignment, and the whole thing collapses to sampling
                      theta and knee from the right shape at initialisation --
                      no settling, no twin, no fifty episodes, on any column of
                      any size. That would be the largest practical result the
                      project has produced, and one seed already points at it,
                      which is exactly why it needs twenty before anyone says so.

  permuting helps     What the one seed shows. If it survives, the settled
                      assignment is actively *worse* than a random one drawn
                      from the same distribution, which would mean homeostasis
                      is fitting each neuron to something that does not
                      generalise -- overfitting, in a thermostat. That reading
                      is strange enough that the first response should be to
                      check the permutation is really being applied, not to
                      write it up.

WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
---------------------
Nothing about *why* a distribution would suffice, if it does. And nothing about
columns of other sizes -- everything here is 96 neurons, and "sample from the
right distribution" is a claim that would need testing at 1024 before it is
worth acting on.

SETTINGS
--------
20 seeds, 100 episodes, checkpoints every 10, 300 probe episodes, 96 neurons,
lr 0, binding and lateral off, `--preset-from 50 --preset-input shuffled`.
Permutation seeded per model seed, so every condition faces one draw and not a
lucky one.

RESULT -- 20 seeds
------------------
condition          final   sparsity
all                0.755    0.030
preset-shuffled    0.803    0.027     (sweep 023)
preset-theta       0.763    0.028
preset-permuted    0.673    0.060
preset-knee        0.566    0.002     <- dead column, uninterpretable

  preset-permuted vs preset-shuffled  final    -0.130   0/20  p = 0.0000
                                      sparsity +0.032  20/20  p = 0.0000
  preset-theta    vs preset-shuffled  final    -0.041   2/20  p = 0.0005
                                      sparsity +0.000  20/20  p = 0.0000
  preset-knee     vs preset-shuffled  final    -0.237   0/20  p = 0.0000
                                      sparsity -0.025   0/20  p = 0.0000

THE SETTLING IS A PER-NEURON FIT, NOT A RECIPE
------------------------------------------------
**Permuting costs 0.130, on 0 of 20 seeds, at p = 0.0000.** The prediction said
"at least 0.04" and it is three times that. Same numbers, wrong owners, and most
of the benefit is gone -- from 0.803 down to 0.673, against a no-preset baseline
of 0.755 and an unsettled column at 0.582.

So the operating point cannot be generated. It is not a distribution to sample
at initialisation; each neuron needs the threshold that was fitted to *its own*
inputs. Sweep 023's "precomputable" is therefore narrower than it sounded:
precomputable from **any input**, including structureless noise, but not without
running the column it belongs to.

And the sparsity column says why, in exactly the way the mechanism predicted.
The permuted column fires at **0.060 against a 0.030 target** -- double rate, on
20 of 20 seeds. Membrane constants are drawn log-uniformly across 12-320ms and
fan-in is sampled per neuron, so a threshold correct for a slow, heavily-driven
unit is badly wrong on a fast, lightly-driven one. Handing each neuron someone
else's threshold does not preserve the population's operating point; it destroys
per-unit rate control while leaving the distribution of values intact.

THE PILOT WAS WRONG AGAIN, AND THE PREDICTION SAID SO IN ADVANCE
------------------------------------------------------------------
One seed said permuting was **free** -- 0.806 permuted against 0.722 unpermuted,
pointing the opposite way. At 20 seeds it is -0.130 on every single seed.

The prediction bet against it explicitly, on mechanism, and recorded the reason:
a single seed had produced three claims that twenty seeds corrected (020's
ordering, 023's sign, 024's magnitude), so it was 0-for-3. **It is now 0-for-4**,
and this is the first time the mechanistic prior was written down in advance and
won. That is the practice working rather than luck: the argument from
log-uniform membrane constants and per-neuron fan-in was available before the
run, and it was right about both the direction and the reason.

WHICH VECTOR CARRIES IT
------------------------
`preset-theta` reaches 0.763 with the knee left at `knee_init`, against 0.803
for both and 0.582 unsettled. So **theta carries +0.181 of the +0.221 and the
knee adds the last +0.041** (p = 0.0005). Sparsity is healthy at 0.028, so that
is a real comparison.

`preset-knee` is **uninterpretable and recorded as such**: with theta left at
`threshold_init` and never adapting, the column sits at sparsity 0.002 -- silent.
Its 0.566 is a fact about a dead column, not about the knee. Third time the
sparsity column has caught this pattern (sweeps 022, 023, now here), which is
why it is printed beside decodability rather than on request.

WHAT THIS SETTLES AND WHAT IT OPENS
-------------------------------------
Settled: the operating point is a per-neuron fit, dominated by the somatic
threshold, and it cannot be sampled or transplanted. The cheap-initialisation
idea is dead -- and it is worth having killed it in one 20-seed sweep rather
than after building it.

Open, and now sharper: **what is theta fitted to?** It is not the firing rate
alone -- sweep 023 showed the condition sitting exactly on target decodes worst
of three, and here the permuted column's rate is *double* target while its
decodability drops, which is consistent but does not identify the quantity. The
direct test is to correlate each neuron's settled theta against its own
measurable properties -- membrane tau, fan-in size, excitatory fraction of its
sources, mean drive -- and see which one predicts it. That is a probe, not a
sweep, and it needs no CI.
