Sweep 007 -- sign-only eligibility with the scaling fixed. 24 neurons, 20 seeds.

Sweep 005 tested sign-only but ran before the elig_rms bug was found, so its
update sizes were mis-scaled like everything before it. Sweep 006 then
re-established the magnitude baseline cleanly and came back null (+0.006,
p = 0.66), which leaves this as the one untested idea derived from a
measurement rather than a guess.

The gradient check says the trace agrees with the true gradient in sign 0.64 of
the time (58/90, p = 0.004) and in magnitude not at all (r = -0.045).
elig_mode="sign" therefore keeps the direction, discards the size, and retains
magnitude only as a participation gate at one RMS.

Prediction, recorded before the result: this is the last cheap idea. If it is
null too, the trace needs rebuilding rather than reinterpreting -- the surrogate
h smooths the threshold so gradients keep flowing, but nothing makes it
proportional to how sensitive a spike count actually is to a weight, and that
is a design change rather than a parameter.

RESULT -- 20 paired seeds, ELIG_MODE=sign confirmed in the run env:
  frozen 0.592 +/- 0.059, plastic 0.595 +/- 0.060, mean paired difference
  +0.003, better on 8/20, permutation p = 0.77.
  The prediction held. Keeping only the sign is not enough, so the trace needs
  rebuilding rather than reinterpreting. Five clean nulls; the cheap ideas are
  exhausted and the surrogate h is the remaining suspect.
