Sweep 025 -- is the column self-organising toward criticality?
==============================================================

QUESTION
--------
The project's own numbers point here and it took an outside framing to see it.
The designed three-factor learning rule is worth -0.003 (p = 0.79). Homeostasis
-- which is not a learning rule but each neuron holding its own activity near a
setpoint -- is worth +0.197. Sweep 023 then found that benefit comes out *better*
from structureless input than from the task, so it is not acquiring the task; it
is finding a regime in which the column becomes readable.

That is learning as a property of the system operating rather than of a
mechanism designated to produce it. It has a name -- self-organised criticality
-- and, unlike "emergent structure", it has a measurement.

A branching process turns one event into `m` further events on average. Below 1
activity dies out; above 1 it explodes; at `m = 1` a network holds and combines
information over the longest timescales available to it. If homeostasis drives
the column toward m = 1, criticality is the mechanism behind the +0.197, and the
thing to optimise becomes "stay critical" rather than "learn well".

This also unblocks TODO item 1's third bullet, which has read "emergent
structure needs a metric before it needs an experiment" since it was written.
`m` is the metric.

TWO WAYS TO GET THIS WRONG, BOTH HANDLED BEFORE ANY CONDITION RAN
-------------------------------------------------------------------
**1. The textbook signature does not apply.** Avalanche-size power laws need
avalanches, and an avalanche is a run of activity bounded by silence. At 96
neurons and 3% sparsity this column emits ~2.9 events per millisecond, so
silence is rare and the entire recording is one avalanche. An exponent computed
from that would be a number produced by a definition that does not hold. The
estimate is therefore the multistep regression of Wilting & Priesemann (2018),
which reads `m` off the decay of the activity autocorrelation and needs no
silent bins.

**2. The estimator measures the wrong thing by default, and by a lot.** It
assumes the autocorrelation comes from propagation. In this model it mostly does
not: membrane time constants run to 320ms, so activity is autocorrelated whether
or not events cause events.

Measured at one seed: raw m = 0.9246. With the recurrent synapses silenced --
same membrane constants, same external drive -- the control reads **0.9145**.
So roughly 99% of the apparent branching is single-neuron leakiness.

**Reporting "this column sits near critical at m = 0.92" would have been a
statement about `tau_soma` and not about the network.** Only the excess over the
control is evidence, and it is +0.0101.

The control has its own confound and it is handled too: silencing recurrence
removes drive, so a naive control runs at a lower rate and its `m` would differ
for that reason as much as for the missing propagation. Homeostasis is therefore
allowed to re-settle afterwards, which restores activity while the recurrence
stays dead -- synaptic scaling is multiplicative, so a zeroed weight stays
zeroed. `sparsity_control` is recorded so the match is checked rather than
assumed, and at one seed it reads 0.0180 against 0.0196.

A BIAS THE ESTIMATOR HAD, CAUGHT BY GROUND TRUTH
--------------------------------------------------
The first version cut the fit at the first non-positive correlation. That is a
selection effect: it keeps only the lags where sampling noise landed above zero,
flattening the tail and shallowing the slope. Against a synthetic process with a
known m of 0.70 it returned **0.7856** -- an 8.5% overestimate, in the direction
that makes any network look closer to critical than it is.

Caught because `test_branching_estimator_recovers_a_known_branching_ratio`
generates a branching process with immigration, whose lag-k correlation is
exactly `m^k`, so the estimator has a right answer to be checked against. That
is stronger than any assertion about the column, where there is no ground truth
and a plausible number would have passed. Now recovers 0.70 -> 0.710,
0.90 -> 0.894, 0.95 -> 0.948, 0.99 -> 0.9901.

CONDITIONS  (20 seeds, 100 settling episodes, 96 neurons, column lr 0)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  all            everything adapting
  no-theta       threshold homeostasis off  -- known to go silent
  no-knee        knee adaptation off
  on             salience-gated Hebbian binding
  both           binding + lateral inhibition

The question is not "what is m" but **does `m_excess` track decodability across
conditions that differ in decodability**. A criticality account requires that it
does.

PREDICTION (recorded before the run)
--------------------------------------
Against my own hypothesis, on the strength of the control.

  all        m_excess +0.005 to +0.020, linear ~0.76
  no-theta   uninterpretable -- sparsity ~0.001, a dead column, recorded as such
  no-knee    m_excess within 0.01 of `all`, linear ~0.70
  on         m_excess within 0.01 of `all`, linear ~0.85
  both       m_excess within 0.01 of `all`, linear ~0.90

**The central claim: `m_excess` stays under 0.03 in every live condition and
does NOT track decodability.** Specifically, `both` should decode ~0.14 higher
than `all` while its branching excess sits within 0.01 of it. If that holds,
criticality is *not* the mechanism behind the +0.197, and the correct reading of
sweeps 022-023 stands unchanged: the emergence is real, but it is an operating
point -- a firing rate per neuron -- and not a critical propagation regime.

The reasoning: at one seed the entire branching contribution is +0.010 against a
membrane contribution of 0.914. A quantity that small has very little room to
explain a +0.197 effect, and the +0.197 was already shown to be reproducible by
copying two per-neuron vectors, which is not what a dynamical regime looks like.

WHAT WOULD CONFIRM THE HYPOTHESIS INSTEAD
-------------------------------------------
`m_excess` ordering the conditions the same way decodability does, with a spread
wide enough to matter -- say `both` at +0.04 against `all` at +0.01. That would
mean the mechanisms this project has been building are working by pushing the
network toward critical propagation, which would reframe the whole programme:
the target becomes a dynamical regime, homeostasis stops being infrastructure
and becomes the main mechanism, and "add another learning rule" stops being the
default move.

I am predicting against it and would find it the more interesting result. Saying
so in advance because it is my own hypothesis and this is exactly the situation
where a sweep gets read generously.

WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY EITHER WAY
--------------------------------
Criticality is one formalisation of "structure emerges from operating" and a
negative here does not close the broader idea -- it closes this measurement of
it. Two other formalisations stay open and are cheap from where this leaves us:
whether the *wiring* self-organises (structural rather than synaptic plasticity,
which `Column.add_inputs` already does mechanically), and whether the operating
point sweeps 022-023 found is itself the emergent structure, in which case the
finding is that the emergence is real and much simpler than criticality.

SETTINGS
--------
20 seeds, 100 settling episodes, 40 episodes of recorded activity, 40 re-settle
episodes for the control, 300 probe episodes, 96 neurons, column lr 0.

THE PREDICTION HOLDS, AND CRITICALITY IS NOT THE MECHANISM
------------------------------------------------------------
Both halves of the central claim are confirmed.

`m_excess` stays under 0.03 in every live condition -- it spans **0.001 to
0.004**, an order of magnitude inside the bound. And **every single m_excess
comparison is null** (p = 0.13 to 0.23) while decodability moves across the same
conditions by 0.121, from 0.716 to 0.837, with three of four comparisons
significant.

Decodability range 0.121. Branching-excess range 0.003. **Forty times smaller,
and none of it significant.**

THE CLEANEST REFUTATION IS `no-knee`
--------------------------------------
Disabling knee adaptation makes the column decode **reliably worse** -- -0.039,
4/20 seeds, p = 0.0130 -- while its raw branching ratio goes **reliably up** --
+0.004, 15/20 seeds, p = 0.0009.

Two significant effects in opposite directions on the same condition. If `m`
tracked representation quality that could not happen. The condition that is
closest to critical is the one that decodes worst.

So: **the +0.197 that homeostatic settling buys is not a critical propagation
regime.** Sweeps 022-023 stand unchanged, and their reading is now the one left
holding: the emergence is real, and it is an operating point -- a firing rate
and a knee per neuron -- which is a much simpler object than a dynamical regime,
and which sweep 022 already showed can be handed over by copying two vectors.

WHAT THE CONTROL SAVED
-----------------------
Raw `m` is 0.922 to 0.926 across every condition, and three of the four raw-`m`
comparisons are significant. Without the control this sweep would have reported
"the column sits at m = 0.92, close to critical, and every mechanism nudges it
closer -- binding p = 0.048, binding+lateral p = 0.014" and that would have read
as strong support for the hypothesis.

It is an artefact. The control -- recurrence silenced, homeostasis re-settled to
matched activity -- reads ~0.91 by itself, so **99% of that number is membrane
leakiness**, and the significant differences in raw `m` are differences in
single-neuron time constants and drive, not in propagation. Once the control is
subtracted, nothing survives.

The `no-knee` row is the proof that this is not over-caution: raw `m` says it is
*more* critical and significantly so, and it decodes worse. Reading raw `m` as
criticality would have produced a confident, significant, backwards conclusion.

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CLOSE
-----------------------------------
Closed: criticality, as measured by branching ratio, is not what homeostasis is
producing and does not explain the +0.197.

Not closed: the broader idea that structure here emerges from the system
operating rather than from a designated learning mechanism. **That idea is in
better shape than ever** -- it is just that the emergent thing is an operating
point, not a critical regime. The evidence for it is unchanged and strong: the
designed three-factor rule is worth -0.003, homeostasis is worth +0.197, and
sweep 023 found that benefit is obtained better from structureless input than
from the task, at p = 0.0032.

Two formalisations remain, and both are cheaper than this one was:

  structural   Does the *wiring* self-organise? `Column.add_inputs` already does
               the mechanics of rewiring; nothing has asked whether rewiring on
               a local rule beats the fixed random graph.
  the simple   Is the operating point itself the emergent structure? Sweeps
  answer       022-023 point straight at it and nobody has tried to characterise
               what theta and knee encode beyond "not a firing rate" (023's
               sparsity column rules that out).

The second is the obvious next move. It is also the one this sweep makes more
interesting rather than less: whatever the settling process finds, it is worth
+0.197, it survives having its input's timing destroyed, and it is *not*
criticality and *not* rate calibration. Two explanations eliminated, and the
quantity is two vectors long.
