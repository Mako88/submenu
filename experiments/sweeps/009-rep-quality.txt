Sweep 009 -- representation quality, plastic vs frozen. 24 neurons, 20 seeds,
graded surrogate. Measures what the column represents, not what the readout
manages to extract.

Six end-to-end sweeps found nothing, including sweep 008 where fixing the
eligibility trace's magnitude (correlation -0.045 -> +0.207) moved accuracy by
-0.001. That result partly falsified its own diagnosis, which is the reason for
this one.

The suspicion is that end-to-end accuracy is the wrong instrument. The online
readout extracts ~0.59 where an offline decoder on the same states reaches
~0.69, and both arms re-fit it every episode, so any column improvement smaller
than that ~0.10 gap cannot show up. Six sweeps may have been measuring through
something too blunt to see the effect.

experiments/compare_step.py decodes the column state offline and bypasses the
readout entirely, which is the quantity the learning rule is supposed to change.
It was built early and dropped once end-to-end accuracy started working -- in
hindsight the wrong call.

One local seed, which is a lead and not a result:
    frozen   linear 0.675   MLP 0.833
    plastic  linear 0.728   MLP 0.930

Prediction, recorded before the sweep: an effect of roughly that size at
20 seeds would be the first positive result for the learning rule in this
project. Three times today a promising single seed has decayed to zero by
twenty, so the honest prior is that this does the same.

RESULT -- 20 paired seeds, measuring the column state directly:
    linear   frozen 0.639 +/- 0.052   plastic 0.621 +/- 0.062
             mean paired difference -0.018, improved on 6/20, p = 0.084
    MLP      frozen 0.891 +/- 0.050   plastic 0.885 +/- 0.061
             mean paired difference -0.006, improved on 10/20, p = 0.643

  The prediction held, and then some. Seed 0 alone had shown 0.675 -> 0.728 on
  linear; across twenty it is -0.018, with 6/20 improving and a p-value trending
  toward the rule being harmful rather than helpful. Fourth time a single seed
  has misled in this project.

  This also refutes the instrument hypothesis that motivated the sweep. Bypassing
  the readout entirely and measuring what the column represents shows no benefit
  either, so the six earlier end-to-end nulls were not an artefact of measuring
  through a blunt readout.

  Note what the frozen numbers say about the opportunity: linear 0.639 against
  MLP 0.891. The information is richly present and poorly linearly accessible, a
  gap of +0.25 that is precisely the job of a learning rule. The rule is not
  merely failing to help at the margin; it is failing at a well-defined task
  with abundant room, and it makes the representation slightly worse.

  Explanations now excluded: the benchmark (001, 002), the teacher (004), the
  update scaling (006), the trace's magnitude noise (007), the surrogate (008),
  and the measuring instrument (009).
