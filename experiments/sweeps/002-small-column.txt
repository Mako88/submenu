Sweep 002 -- plastic vs frozen at 24 neurons, 20 seeds, 400 episodes.

Sweep 001 (96 neurons) found no effect, but it had no headroom: a random column
of that size already makes delayed XOR 0.86 linearly decodable against 0.98 for
an MLP on the same states, so the rule was asked to improve something already
near its ceiling.

Smaller columns mix less. At 24 neurons the label is clearly present but not
linearly accessible -- linear 0.689 against MLP 0.881, a gap of +0.193. Closing
that gap is precisely what a three-factor rule is for, so this is the first
comparison with room for an effect to exist.

Each file added under experiments/sweeps/ starts one run and records what it
was asking. Sweep 001 lives in experiments/run-experiment.txt.

RESULT -- 20 paired seeds: frozen 0.592 +/- 0.059, plastic 0.602 +/- 0.069,
  mean paired difference +0.011, better on 11/20, permutation p = 0.45.
  No effect, in the regime with the most headroom available. Taken with sweep
  001 (no headroom, -0.003, p = 0.79), this rules out the benchmark being the
  problem and points at the rule or the credit signal it is given.
